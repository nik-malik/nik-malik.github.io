    • AI Risks: Feedback Loops, asymmetric information, biases, and strategic manipulation
    • Algorithmic pricing
  • Digital Platforms
    • Blockchains (Polymarket, Bitcoin, NFT), Housing (Zillow), Creative Content (TikTok, iTunes), SuperApps

Methods: Econometrics · Machine learning · Game Theory
